The game

Cities grow only if you keep them supplied

You don't zone districts or lay out neighborhoods. You run a holding company that builds, buys and upgrades production sites — and the cities around you grow as a consequence of how well you serve them.

Day 1 Day 1000

Same map, same view, one thousand in-game days apart. Drag to compare.

Supply chains

Every building turns inputs into outputs. Break a link and the shelves go empty.

Living cities

Cities grow and shrink on their own, based on the service they get.

Price competition

The cheapest supplier wins the demand. Set your margins building by building.
